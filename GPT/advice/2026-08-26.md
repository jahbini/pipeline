## Known temperamental spots

- **pnpm install destroys the writer→pipeline symlink.** If someone runs
  `pnpm install` in writer, `~/writer/node_modules/@jahbini/pipeline`
  will be reset back to the `.pnpm/<hash>/...` dir. Re-run the symlink
  step from `GPT/mac-mini-sync.md`.
- **UI restart needed after `ui_server.coffee` edits.** All other edits
  (model classes, meta devices, step scripts) are picked up on the next
  pipeline launch automatically.
- **`model.language_model.norm.weight` +1 fold is empirically excluded.**
  Reference `Qwen3NextRMSNorm` says it should be included but we saw
  quality regression when we tried. See `GPT/qwen3_5.md` § "Also worth
  revisiting one day".
- **Base models at temperature=0 loop.** Behavior, not a bug. Use
  temperature 0.3+ for varied output.
